# Goal module: hypertrophy

For muscle size and physique goals.

## Making it measurable

Muscle gain is slow and hard to measure, so track several proxies:
- rep PRs at fixed loads, and e1RM on key lifts (`e1rm:<EXERCISE>`)
- `body:weight_kg` trend (lean gain: about 0.25-0.5%/month for intermediates)
- monthly tape measurements (arm, chest, waist, thigh) and photos, logged as observations
- weekly hard sets per muscle (`training_summary` -> `sets_per_muscle_per_week`)

## Role: Hypertrophy Specialist

Owns:
- weekly hard sets per muscle: start about 10-12 and adjust from recovery
  and progress, usually within 6-20
- proximity to failure: most sets at 1-3 RIR, isolation work can go to 0-1
- exercise selection: stable, loadable in the lengthened position, joint-friendly
- priority muscles: 2-3 at a time get extra volume, the rest maintain

## Structure

- Train each muscle 2x/week or more; upper/lower, push/pull/legs or full
  body depending on days available.
- Rep ranges roughly 5-30. Mostly 6-15 on compounds, 10-20 on isolation.
- Mesocycles of 4-6 weeks: start at about 2-3 RIR, add sets or reps, finish
  near 0-1 RIR, then deload (half the sets, 1 week).
- Double progression: reach the top of the rep range at the target RIR on
  all sets, then add the smallest buildable load (`round_load`).

## Nutrition

Small surplus (about +5-10% of maintenance), protein about 1.6-2.2 g/kg/day.
In a deficit, keep volume and intensity and expect maintenance, not growth,
unless the athlete is a beginner.

## Warning signs

Performance down across 2+ sessions for the same muscle, persistent joint
aches, or readiness factors trending worse: cut volume by 20-30% before
adding anything.
