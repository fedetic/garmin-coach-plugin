# Goal module: general fitness / hybrid / health

For "get fitter", health and longevity, hybrid (strength plus endurance), or
returning after time off.

## Making it measurable

Turn the vague goal into 2-4 concrete markers the athlete cares about, at
least one of them from Garmin:

| Capacity | Example marker |
|---|---|
| Aerobic | `health:vo2max` up X points; run 5k without stopping; Z2 pace at the same HR improving |
| Strength | goblet squat / push-up / pull-up rep targets; `e1rm:` on 1-2 lifts |
| Activity | `health:steps` weekly average; Garmin weekly intensity minutes (`get_weekly_intensity_minutes`, WHO guideline 150 min moderate) |
| Recovery/health | `health:rhr_bpm` trending down; sleep consistency |
| Body | `body:weight_kg` or waist trend if wanted |
| Mobility | touch toes, deep squat hold 60s, overhead reach |

## Role: Health Coach

Owns consistency and sustainability: the best program is the one that gets
done. It balances strength, aerobic work and mobility, and keeps sessions
within the athlete's time and enjoyment.

## Structure

- Minimum effective dose: 2 full-body strength sessions plus 2-3 aerobic
  sessions (mostly Z2, one harder) per week; daily steps.
- Strength: 1-3 compound movements per pattern (squat, hinge, push, pull,
  carry), 2-4 sets, RPE 6-8, simple double progression.
- Returning after time off: first 2-4 weeks at RPE 5-7 and reduced volume.
  Joints and tendons lag behind the cardiovascular system.
- Blocks of 4-8 weeks with a small focus shift each time, to keep it engaging.
- Adherence matters more than optimization here. A missed week is normal;
  restart the plan rather than doubling up.
